Strategic Context & H2 Outlook
Why we're here and where we're headed
🔄 Shift in Expectations

FY26 target assumed a slow loss ramp in Q1/Q4. We strategically accelerated the Mid-Market rollout and customer benefit initiatives into H1, effectively inverting the planned ramp. This front-loaded investment drove growth but also amplified near-term loss exposure.

📉 H2 Trajectory

The team has integrated early learnings from the accelerated rollout. We are actively deploying targeted mitigation initiatives to pull back losses in H2 and drive the run-rate back down toward the 8.9 bps Full Year Target.

🎯 The Three Macro Buckets
1. Ecosystem Fraud — Account Takeover & Synthetic accounts across the Intuit ecosystem.
2. Growth Push — Higher limits, new payment methods, and reduced insults across all products.
3. Broken Experiences — Fixing friction points that cause massive customer churn, but temporarily elevate exposure.
